Who We Are

Building a Community of Tech Enthusiasts

The UniTech Club is a student-run organization dedicated to fostering technical skills, innovation, and career development among students in all academic fields.

Whether you're a beginner looking to write your first line of code or an advanced student looking to collaborate on real-world software projects, our club provides workshops, hackathons, and networking opportunities for everyone.

Everything you need to know about joining the UniTech Club

Membership is open to all currently enrolled university students across all majors. Whether you are a computer science student or simply interested in technology, you are welcome to join.

No prior experience is necessary! We host beginner workshops that start from the basics.

We usually meet twice a week for workshops and group projects.

Joining the club is completely free for all university students.
